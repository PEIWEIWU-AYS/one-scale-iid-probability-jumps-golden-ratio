# Related results and search coverage

Zaytsev and Bellec defined the compact one-scale iid problem and obtained a construction for the unrestricted optimum in Proposition 6.1. Sections 6.1.4 and 6.1.5 give infinite sequences of thresholds for specified winning patterns. Section 7.1.3 asks for infinitely many discontinuities of the unrestricted function. Section 7.3 asks about equality between that function and the transformed one-scale optimum. The main theorem here proves that the optimized one-scale Borel supremum has infinitely many genuine right jumps approaching the golden ratio. Every candidate in both families of §§6.1.4–6.1.5 gives a genuine one-scale jump, and these are all the jumps in (1,φ). The four-mass reduction proves that every rank increment increases the optimum. The one-scale value at φ is 1/4, a supremum never attained by any admissible one-scale law. The finite cubic on both canonical ladders gives the exact optimum below φ. The explicit lower bound (√61−5)/6 for the unrestricted value at φ also follows by taking the long-ladder limit in the original paper and using its Proposition 6.1. The original paper already supplies a two-atom lower construction to its right. The pair-separation estimate for arbitrary laws and its sharp first two uniform coefficients provide a distinct upper comparison. The unrestricted infinitude and the equality question remain open. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.29223v1) · [Original authors’ exact certificates](https://github.com/antalez/flag-algebras-iid)

The endpoint analysis uses the ladder construction in §6.1.7. Bellec and Fritz gave the equal-weight success formula at the endpoint for the reflected problem in Theorem 1.5. The upper bound here partitions the draws below the maximum and applies a sum-of-squares inequality to those ranks. This yields the order of the endpoint deficit uniformly over all Borel laws. [Bellec and Fritz](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.15179v5) · [Zaytsev and Bellec](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.29223v1#S6.SS1.SSS7)

Wu and Peng’s earlier paper restricts *where* a positive discontinuity of the unrestricted function can be, conditional on its existence. Its sampling lemmas are imported at a pinned release. It does not establish the one-scale infinitude or either side of the present unrestricted logarithmic estimate. [Spectral restrictions and proof](https://github.com/PEIWEIWU-AYS/iid-extremal-probability-jumps-spectral-radii/releases/tag/v1.0.0)
